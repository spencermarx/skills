How the depot runs today

Ardoz Depot moves 84,200 parcels on an average weekday, across three shifts and 24 chutes. Every one of them passes a single induction arch, and for 214 minutes of every night that arch is the whole depot.

Three routes considered

Each route was replayed against the same 30 days of scan records. One adds capacity at the arch, one takes work away from it, and one returns the time the arch spends on its own mistakes.

Record: peak hour dwell under each route

Route A: a second induction arch

  • weigh the cost
  • capital works
  • 11 months
Adds A second arch against the west wall, fed directly by the trunk dock.
Buys Peak-hour dwell of 74 minutes, down from 148, and no minute of the night at arch capacity.
Costs Eleven months and structural work on the west wall.
  • Combined rating 18,800 an hour
  • Peak demand 11,300 an hour
  • Bay doors free at 05:10 today

The bays would need to be free by 04:20, and they carry no scan of their own — so the constraint moves somewhere the depot cannot see it.

Route B: pre-sorted trunk containers

  • carry forward
  • process change
  • 6 weeks per depot
Removes 15,700 trunk parcels a night from the arch, 38% of night volume.
Buys Peak-hour arch demand of 7,000 an hour, under the 9,400 rating, and dwell of 96 minutes.
Costs No capital work. Six weeks per origin depot to change the loading standard.
  • BIL-04
  • VIT-02
  • LOG-01
  • PMP-03
  • Arch night load 41,200 to 25,500
  • Handlings per trunk parcel 5 to 2
  • Nothing is built

A container loaded to the wrong chute group is unsortable at the spur: it returns to the arch whole, and costs more arch time than sending it there would have.

Route C: rebuild the exception loop

  • software

Changes A re-labelled parcel rejoins at the chute spur instead of at the head of the arch queue.
Buys 26 minutes of arch time a night, a tenth of its running time, and dwell of 141 minutes.
Costs One release of the sorter control software. Nothing on the floor moves.
  • 2,900 parcels leave the line nightly
  • 1,180 of those need a third pass
  • Seven minutes off the peak

What to take away

The arch is the depot's only constraint, and Route B is the only route that takes work off it rather than building capacity around it: 15,700 parcels a night stop passing it, at no capital cost, and the constraint stays where the depot already measures it.
